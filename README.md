  <h1>Privacy Policy for Star Keeper</h1>
  <p class="meta">Last updated: 3 August 2026</p>

  <div class="callout">
    <strong>In short: Star Keeper does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.</strong>
    There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The game
    does not send information anywhere.
  </div>

  <p>
    This policy explains how the Star Keeper mobile game ("the game", "we") handles information.
    It applies to the Android application published under the package name
    <code>com.haloudba.starkeeper</code>.
  </p>

  <h2>Information we collect</h2>
  <p><strong>None.</strong> We do not collect, request, or receive any personal or sensitive
  user data. Specifically, the game does not collect:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact information</li>
    <li>Location data of any kind</li>
    <li>Photos, files, contacts, calendar entries, or microphone or camera input</li>
    <li>Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage analytics</li>
    <li>Crash reports or diagnostic telemetry</li>
  </ul>
  <p>We do not ask you to create an account or sign in, and the game contains no advertising
  and no third-party analytics or tracking software.</p>

  <h2>Information stored on your device</h2>
  <p>
    The game saves your progress — such as your town layout, current galaxy, level, and
    in-game currency balances — using your device's local storage. This information:
  </p>
  <ul>
    <li>never leaves your device;</li>
    <li>is not transmitted to us or to any third party;</li>
    <li>is not accessible to us in any form;</li>
    <li>is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its app data.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Network access</h2>
  <p>
    The Android application declares the <code>INTERNET</code> permission because it is
    required by the underlying application framework. The game itself makes no network
    requests: all graphics, fonts, sounds, and game logic are bundled inside the app and it
    is fully playable offline.
  </p>

  <h2>Purchases</h2>
  <p>
    Star Keeper may offer optional in-app purchases of virtual in-game items. Any such
    purchase is processed entirely by <strong>Google Play Billing</strong>. Payment details
    are handled by Google and are never sent to, seen by, or stored by us. Please refer to
    <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy">Google's Privacy Policy</a> for details of
    how Google processes payment information. Refunds are subject to Google Play's refund
    policy.
  </p>

  <h2>Children's privacy</h2>
  <p>
    Star Keeper is suitable for a general audience. Because the game collects no data
    whatsoever, it collects no data from children. We do not knowingly collect personal
    information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
  </p>

  <h2>Data sharing and selling</h2>
  <p>
    We do not share, sell, rent, or trade user information, because we do not collect any.
  </p>

  <h2>Your rights</h2>
  <p>
    Since no personal data is collected or held by us, there is no personal data to access,
    correct, export, or delete. To remove all locally stored game progress, clear the app's
    data in your device settings or uninstall the game.
  </p>

  <h2>Changes to this policy</h2>
  <p>
    If the game's data practices change in a future version, this policy will be updated and
    the "last updated" date above will be revised. Material changes will be described here.
  </p>
